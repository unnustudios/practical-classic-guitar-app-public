Practical Classic Guitar

Classical guitar, every day. A short, complete practice session, ready when you open the app, on iPad.

What it does

  • Today's session: a warm-up, technique for both hands, scales, and the pieces you are learning, in the time you have, from 10 to 45 minutes.
  • Every exercise, clearly shown: as tab and on the fretboard, with a metronome that plays along. Say how it felt, and the tempo goes up when it is easy and back down when it is hard.
  • Your pieces come back at the right time: add them section by section. Each section returns a day later, then after two days, four, a week, two weeks and a month.
  • A tuner and trainers: a tuner that listens to your guitar, a note trainer, Find the note, an interval trainer for your ear, and a map of the whole neck.
  • A quiet history: the days you practiced and the minutes you played. No streaks to lose and no points.

Yours, on your iPad

No account and no ads. Your practice stays on your iPad and the app collects nothing. The tuner listens only while it is open, and never records. The privacy policy has the details.
