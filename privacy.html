Practical Classic Guitar

Privacy policy

Practical Classic Guitar. Effective 16 November 2026.

The short version: the app collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server to send anything to.

What stays on your iPad

Your practice history, your pieces, your settings and your results in the trainers are kept on your iPad, inside the app. We never see them. They are deleted when you delete the app. Like any app's data, they are part of your iPad's backups to iCloud or to a computer, if you make them.

The microphone

The tuner and "Find the note" listen to your guitar, and only while one of them is listening on screen. The sound is analysed as it arrives and is never recorded, kept or sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off in the iPad's Settings at any time, and the rest of the app works without it.

The practice reminder

If you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled on your iPad. It does not come from a server.

The voice in "Find the note"

It is your iPad's own speech, made on the device.

When something goes wrong

The app writes a short line to the iPad's own system log, saying what could not be done. It never includes anything you typed or played. The log stays on your iPad and the app does not send it.

Crash reports and usage from Apple

If you chose, in your iPad's Settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage figures for this app with us. That comes from Apple, not from the app, and you can turn it off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers.

If you write to us

"Write to us" in the app's Settings opens an email in your own mail app. It has a line of details filled in: the app's version, the iPadOS version, the kind of iPad and the app's language. You can read it, change it or delete it before you send. If you send it, we get your message, your email address and whatever the message contains. We use them only to answer you and to make the app better. We do not sell them or share them with anyone, and we delete them when you ask.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it kept on your iPad goes with it. To have a message you sent us deleted, write to the address below.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
