Support

Practical Classic Guitar is a daily practice companion for classical guitar, on iPad.

Write to us at contact@unnustudios.com, or from the app: Settings, then "Write to us". We read every message, in English or Portuguese.

Common questions

The tuner or "Find the note" does not hear my guitar

They need the microphone. If you said no when the app asked, open your device's Settings, find Practical Classic Guitar and turn Microphone on. Play close to the device, one string at a time.

The practice reminder does not show

The app needs permission to send notifications. In your device's Settings, find Practical Classic Guitar, then Notifications, and allow them. The reminder is skipped on days you have already practiced. If you don't open the app for a week, it goes quiet, and opening the app starts it again.

Where is my practice history kept?

Only on your device, inside the app. There is no account and no sync between devices. To move it to a new device, restore it from a backup of the old one, to iCloud or to a computer.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything it kept goes with it.

What does it need?

An iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.
